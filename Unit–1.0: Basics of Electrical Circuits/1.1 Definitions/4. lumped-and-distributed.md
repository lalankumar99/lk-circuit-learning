# Lumped and Distributed Components

## 1. Lumped Component

A **lumped component** is an electrical component whose physical size is very small compared to the wavelength or dimensions of the circuit.

Its electrical parameters such as **R, L, and C** are considered concentrated at a single point.

### Examples

- Resistor (R)
- Capacitor (C)
- Inductor (L)
- Transformer (T)

### Lumped Component Circuit Example

**R, L, and C are represented as individual components at specific points in the circuit.**

## 2. Distributed Component

A **distributed component** is an electrical component or system in which the electrical parameters such as **R, L, and C** are distributed continuously along its physical length.

### Examples

- Transmission Line
- Coaxial Cable
- Microstrip Line
- Waveguide

![Distributed Component](https://lalankumar99.github.io/lk-circuit-learning/Icons/Diagram/distributed.png)

### Distributed Component Circuit Example

**R, L, and C are distributed along the length of the transmission line.**

---

## Difference Between Lumped and Distributed Components

| Lumped Component | Distributed Component |
|---|---|
| Parameters are concentrated at specific points. | Parameters are distributed along the physical length. |
| Physical size is small compared to the wavelength. | Physical dimensions can be comparable to the wavelength. |
| R, L, and C are treated as individual elements. | R, L, and C are distributed continuously. |
| Common in low-frequency circuits. | Important in transmission-line and high-frequency circuits. |
| Examples: R, C, L, Transformer | Examples: Transmission Line, Coaxial Cable, Microstrip Line, Waveguide |

---

## Quick Revision

**Lumped → Parameters concentrated at a point → R, C, L**

**Distributed → Parameters distributed along length → Transmission Line**
