# Short Circuit

A **short circuit** is an unintended low-resistance path between two points of an electrical circuit.

Because the resistance of the path is very low, a **very large current** can flow through the circuit.

### Basic Concept

**Very Low Resistance Path → Very High Current**

![Short Circuit](https://lalankumar99.github.io/lk-circuit-learning/Icons/Diagram/short-circuit.png)

### Example

A direct connection between the positive and negative terminals of a voltage source can create a **short circuit**.

### Key Points

- Provides an unintended low-resistance path.
- Can cause very high current to flow.
- Can damage circuit components and wiring.
- A fuse or circuit breaker can help protect a circuit from excessive current.
- The resistance of an ideal short circuit is considered **zero**.

### Quick Revision

**Short Circuit → Very Low Resistance → Very High Current**
